Natural Wake Up privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Natural Wake Up is made by Stillmoth LLC. It collects no personal information.

  • Location. If you allow it, the app reads your location once to work out sunrise times. You can type a city instead. Your location stays on your phone.
  • Weather. For the cloud forecast, the app sends your location, rounded to about 1 km, to MET Norway or the US National Weather Service, whichever you choose. The service can see your IP address. You can turn the forecast off in Settings.
  • Settings stay on your phone and are deleted with the app. Android may include them in your phone’s backup.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Tips go through Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details.
