Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Stillmoth apps do not collect personal information. We have no servers that receive your data.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings are stored on your device and are deleted with the app, unless your phone’s own backup keeps a copy.
  • Tips are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your name or payment details.
  • Permissions, such as notifications, are used only on your device to make the app work.
  • Email you send us is used only to reply to you.
  • This website uses no cookies or analytics. Our host may keep standard server logs, and fonts load from Google Fonts.

Sunrise Wake-Up

  • Location. If you allow it, the app reads your phone’s location once to work out sunrise times for where you are. You can type a city instead. Place names come from a list built into the app, so your location is not sent anywhere to look up a name.
  • Weather. To show the cloud forecast, the app sends your location, rounded to about 1 km, to a public weather service: MET Norway (the default) or the US National Weather Service, whichever you choose. Like any website, the service can see your device’s IP address. Nothing else is sent, and no account or name is involved. You can turn the forecast off in Settings.
  • Backup. On Android, your settings and saved location can be included in your phone’s backup to your Google account, so they come back if you reinstall the app. You control this in your phone’s backup settings.

If this changes, this page will be updated first.
